Anchor & verify

A watchlist match doesn't just get logged — it gets sealed: hashed, pinned to IPFS, and written to a Polygon smart contract. Anyone can later re-upload the same clip and the app checks it two ways — against the local record and against the on-chain one — so verification never has to take the repo's word for it.
